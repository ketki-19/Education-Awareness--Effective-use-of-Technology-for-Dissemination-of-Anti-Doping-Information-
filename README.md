# Education-Awareness--Effective-use-of-Technology-for-Dissemination-of-Anti-Doping-Information-
This project aims to spread awareness about doping, prohibited substances, and the importance of fair play in sports through modern technology. Doping refers to the use of banned substances or methods to improve athletic performance artificially. It can negatively affect athletes' health, careers, and sports integrity. 
Education and Awareness – Effective Use of Technology for Dissemination of Anti-Doping Information is a technology-based project designed to spread knowledge and awareness about doping, prohibited substances, and the importance of fair play in sports. Doping refers to the use of banned substances or methods by athletes to improve their performance artificially. It can cause serious health problems, damage an athlete's career, and affect the fairness and integrity of sports.
The main objective of this project is to provide athletes, coaches, and sports professionals with accurate, updated, and easily accessible anti-doping information through a digital platform. The system uses modern technologies such as web development, databases, and Artificial Intelligence (AI) to make learning more interactive, effective, and user-friendly.
